Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Applies to the feedback form at feedback.dic.ac and the feedback button inside Limpox apps and websites

The short version

We only receive what you send in the feedback form, plus basic technical details that help us fix problems. Name and email are optional. We use it all to handle your feedback and improve our products. We don’t sell it, don’t use it for ads and don’t track you across sites.

What we collect

  • What you write: the type of feedback, title, details and, for bugs, how bad it is.
  • Optional contact details: your name and email, only if you enter them.
  • Files you attach: screenshots or other files (up to 3, 5 MB each).
  • Technical details: browser and operating system, language, time zone, screen and window size, and the address of the page you sent it from, without the part after “?” (which can contain personal data). The form shows you these details before you send.
  • App details: which app or website the feedback is about, its version, and any reference the app added (for example an order or screen name).
  • Connection details: your IP address and browser identifier, to stop spam and abuse.
  • Your agreement: if the form asks you to accept the terms, the time you accepted them.

How we use it

To read, sort and fix what you report, to reply if you left an email, to improve our apps and websites, and to protect the form from spam and abuse. We don’t use it for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

Only the Limpox team that maintains the app you wrote about. Your feedback is stored on servers of our hosting provider (Hostinger), which processes it on our behalf. We don’t sell or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Cookies

The feedback form doesn’t use cookies or trackers. Inside a website, the form runs separately from the site. It loads its settings and translations from our server when it opens, but nothing you type or attach is sent until you press Send.

How long we keep it

IP addresses are deleted automatically after 90 days. Feedback, files and contact details are kept while they help us support and improve the app. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can send feedback without a name or email. You can ask to see, correct or delete the feedback you sent: email us with the reference number shown after sending (for example FB-…). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object, to restrict use, or to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The feedback form is not directed at children under 13. If a child sent us personal information, ask us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email rotem+Feedback@limpox.co.il.
