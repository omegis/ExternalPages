Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Applies to Sudori for iPhone and iPad

The short version

Sudori has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We don't run servers that receive your data. We can't see your games, scores or settings.

What stays on your device

Your games, statistics, streak, coins and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, a copy syncs through your own iCloud (Apple's key-value storage), which only your Apple Account can access. We have no access to it.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Sudori submits your best winning times to Game Center leaderboards. Apple runs Game Center, and other players can see your Game Center name and times there, under Apple's privacy policy. If you're not signed in, nothing is submitted.

Notifications

If you turn on Streak Reminder, Sudori schedules a reminder on your device. No data leaves the device to send it.

Sharing

When you share a puzzle or a result, Sudori creates a short text with the puzzle code. It's sent only where you choose to share it.

Data we collect

None. Sudori does not collect personal data as defined by Apple's App Privacy guidelines, and contains no third-party SDKs.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes the data on that device. Turning off iCloud sync for your Apple Account stops syncing. Game Center scores can be managed in the iOS Settings app under Game Center.

Children

Sudori is suitable for all ages. It has no chat, no user accounts and no advertising, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

מדיניות פרטיות — בקצרה

ב־Sudori אין חשבונות, פרסומות, אנליטיקה או מעקב. איננו מפעילים שרתים שמקבלים את הנתונים שלך. המשחקים, הסטטיסטיקה וההגדרות נשמרים במכשיר וב־iCloud הפרטי שלך. אם התחברת ל־Game Center, הזמנים הטובים ביותר שלך נשלחים לטבלאות של Game Center שמופעל על ידי Apple.
