Support

Is Sudori really free?

Yes. Every mode, level, style and lesson is free. There are no ads, no purchases and no account.

Move to a new iPhone or iPad

With iCloud turned on, your games, settings, streak and coins sync through your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new device and open Sudori.

Move to a new Android phone

Sudori for Android keeps your games on the device and in your own Google backup. When you set up the new phone with the same Google Account and restore from backup, install Sudori from Google Play and your games, settings, streak and coins come back. Games don't move between iPhone and Android.

My streak broke

Every first win of a day earns coins. On the streak page (tap the flame on Home) you can spend 50 coins to buy back a missed day from the last 14 days, which repairs the streak.

Leaderboards don't show my time

On iPhone and iPad, best times go to Game Center. Check that you're signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. Leaderboards rank Classic wins at each level. A game where you used a hint, or continued after running out of mistakes, isn't ranked. Sudori for Android has no leaderboards; your best times are in the Me tab.

Turn off the music or sounds

Tap the speaker icon at the top of the game screen, or open Settings → Audio Effects. Sudori also follows your iPhone's silent switch, and on Android your media volume.

Share a puzzle

Every puzzle has a code. Use the share button in a game, and your friend can play it from the Race tab under Play a friend's puzzle.
