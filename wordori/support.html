Wordori Support

How do I play?

Drag your finger across the letters to spell a word. Words can turn corners but never go diagonally. Every letter belongs to exactly one hidden word; find them all to complete the grid. You can also tap letters one at a time.

How do I change the word language?

Tap the language button on the home screen, or open Settings › Word language. The app's menus follow your device language (on Android 13 and later you can also choose the app's language in the phone's settings).

What are coins for?

You earn coins by finishing levels and daily puzzles. Spend them on hints: show where a word starts, or reveal a whole word. You can also buy coin packs: tap the coin counter.

I bought coins. Where are they?

iPhone and iPad: coins are added as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. If a purchase needs approval (Ask to Buy), they arrive once it is approved, even if the app was closed. Coins are kept on this device only: they don't move to another device and are lost if you delete the app. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Android: coins are added as soon as Google Play confirms the purchase. A purchase that is still pending (for example a cash payment or a family approval) pays when it completes; open the app afterwards. Coins are included in your phone's Android backup, so they come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with backup turned on. For refunds, use your Google Play order history.

How does the daily puzzle work?

Every day there is one new puzzle per language, the same for every player. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

I found a wrong or strange word

Please tell us the word, the word language and the level number, and we'll fix it in the next update.
