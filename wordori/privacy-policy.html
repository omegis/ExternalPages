Wordori Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Wordori is a word puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. It works fully offline and has no account system. The only thing it ever sends is feedback that you choose to send us.

Data we collect

Nothing, unless you send us feedback. Wordori contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and never sells or shares data.

Feedback ("Something not right?")

In a level you can open More › Something not right? and send us a short report. Only when you press Send, the app sends to our feedback service (feedback.dic.ac, run by us):

  • what you chose (for example "Wrong or missing word") and the note you wrote, if any;
  • your email address, only if you enter one, so we can reply;
  • the level you were playing (its number or daily date, word language, theme and how many words you had found);
  • the app version, your device type (for example "iPhone17,2" or "Samsung SM-N986B") and the iOS or Android version.

The service also records the time and the IP address the report came from, to stop abuse. No progress, coins, purchases or device identifiers are sent. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve the game, and to answer you if you left an email. We do not share it. To have a report deleted, write to us at the address below.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles the payment; we receive no payment details or personal data, only their confirmation that a purchase happened, which the app checks on your device.

Data on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, daily puzzles, statistics) and settings are saved only on your device. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes them. On Android, they are included in your phone's own Android backup (to your Google account, if you have backup turned on), so they can come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone; we never see that backup.

Links

The app can open the store page of our other game (App Store or Google Play) and this website. Those pages are run by Apple, Google and GitHub under their own policies.

Children

Wordori is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, including children, unless they send us feedback; please do not include personal details in a feedback note.
