Wordori Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Wordori is a word puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. It works fully offline and has no account system.

Data we collect

None. Wordori does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles the payment; we receive no payment details or personal data, only their confirmation that a purchase happened, which the app checks on your device.

Data on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, daily puzzles, statistics) and settings are saved only on your device. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes them. On Android, they are included in your phone's own Android backup (to your Google account, if you have backup turned on), so they can come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone; we never see that backup.

Links

The app can open the store page of our other game (App Store or Google Play) and this website. Those pages are run by Apple, Google and GitHub under their own policies.

Children

Wordori is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
