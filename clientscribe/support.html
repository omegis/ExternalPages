Subscriptions

  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. After the trial, the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • To manage or cancel, open ClientScribe › Settings › Manage subscription, or go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.
  • To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings › Restore purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

  • Transcription isn't available for my language. ClientScribe only uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads its speech model; keep the app open and connected to Wi-Fi. If your device has no on-device model for that language, choose another session language.
  • The notes weren't written by Apple Intelligence. Apple Intelligence must be turned on in iOS Settings and must support the session's language. Otherwise ClientScribe drafts notes with its own on-device summarizer. Please review them before use.
  • Moving to a new device. In Settings › Export encrypted backup, create a password-protected backup. On the new device, use Settings › Restore from backup. Without the password the backup cannot be opened, including by us.
  • Microphone access. If recording won't start, turn on the microphone for ClientScribe in iOS Settings › ClientScribe.
  • Recording consent. Settings › Recording consent controls whether ClientScribe asks you to log consent before each recording. You are responsible for following the recording rules where you practise.
