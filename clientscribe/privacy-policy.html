ClientScribe Privacy Policy

ClientScribe: Private AI Notes · Effective 2 November 2026

ClientScribe does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data or your clients' data. Recordings, transcripts and notes are created and stored on your device. We have no servers that receive them.

Who we are

ClientScribe is published by Bar Tech Labs Limited ("we", "us"). You can reach us at rotem+ClientScribe@limpox.co.il.

What stays on your device

  • Client information. Client names, contact details, matters, session titles, recordings, transcripts, notes, action items and tags are stored only in the app's library on your device. The library uses iOS Data Protection.
  • Audio. You choose, per client, whether audio is never written to disk, deleted right after transcription, or kept. If audio is kept, it is encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM) with a key held in the device's Keychain.
  • Transcription. Speech is transcribed by Apple's on-device speech recognition. ClientScribe never uses server-based speech recognition.
  • Notes. Notes and client summaries are written on the device by Apple Intelligence where it is available and supports the language, or otherwise by ClientScribe's own on-device summarizer.
  • Search. The search index is part of the local library and never leaves the device.
  • Speaker identification. After a recording, ClientScribe works out on the device who spoke ("Me", the client or other speakers), using open-source models built into the app. It analyses a temporary copy of the audio and deletes it right after. Nothing is downloaded or sent. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Lock Screen. While you record, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show a timer and, unless you turn it off in Settings, the client's name. No audio or transcript is shown there, but people near your phone can see it.

What we collect

Nothing. ClientScribe has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only third-party code is open-source software for on-device processing, which sends nothing. We do not receive your recordings, transcripts, notes or any information about your clients.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to send or save it:

  • Email. When you email notes or a client summary, the message is created in Apple's Mail composer and sent from your own email account. ClientScribe does not see or store sent messages.
  • Exports. PDF, Word (DOCX) and CSV files are created on the device and go wherever you share or save them. A PDF can optionally be password-protected.
  • Encrypted backups. A ClientScribe backup is created only when you ask for one. It is encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you choose. Without that password it cannot be opened, including by us.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup can include ClientScribe's library, as for other apps. Device backups are handled by Apple or by your computer, under their terms. We have no access to them.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record sessions you start.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe on the device.
  • Face ID / passcode: optional, to lock the app. Biometric data never reaches the app; iOS only tells it whether unlocking succeeded.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple may share limited, non-identifying transaction information with us (such as whether a subscription is active), as described in Apple's privacy policy. No client information is ever attached to a purchase.

Your clients' data and your responsibilities

You decide what to record and keep, and you remain responsible for that information under the laws and professional rules that apply to you. These may include obtaining consent to record, confidentiality and record-keeping duties. ClientScribe can show a consent step before each recording and log when consent was given. Because we never receive this data, requests from your clients to access or delete their information should be handled by you on your device.

Deleting data

Deleting a session or a client in ClientScribe removes it from the device, and the library overwrites deleted content. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. Copies you exported, emailed or included in backups are not affected. To remove those, delete them where they are stored.

AI-generated content

Transcripts and notes are generated automatically and can contain mistakes. Please review them before relying on them or sending them. ClientScribe is not legal advice, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional judgment.

Children

ClientScribe is intended for professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
