Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Applies to TimetoEat for iPhone and Apple Watch

The short version

TimetoEat has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We don't run servers that receive your data. We can't see your fasts, your health answers or your Apple Health data.

What stays on your device

Your fasts, health-check answers and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only you can access. We have no access to it.

Apple Health

With your permission, TimetoEat reads steps, active energy, sleep, blood glucose and weight to personalize your plan, and saves glucose or weight values you choose to enter. Health data is processed on your device only. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never transmitted to us.

Motion

If Apple Health has no step data, TimetoEat may count today's steps with your device's motion sensor. This stays on your device.

AI Coach

The AI Coach runs entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence (Apple’s on-device language model). Your fasting data is never sent to us or to any server to generate it.

Purchases

TimetoEat Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we receive no payment details.

Data we collect

None. TimetoEat does not collect personal data as defined by Apple's App Privacy guidelines, and contains no third-party SDKs.

Your choices

You can revoke Health or Motion access at any time in the iOS Settings app. In TimetoEat, Settings → Delete all data permanently removes everything the app stored on your device and in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes local data.

Children

TimetoEat is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
