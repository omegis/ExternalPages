How does the Smart Path decide?

After each fast it looks at your last four fasts at your current target. Three completed fasts move you up a step. Fasts ending early twice in a row, or a fast that felt very hard, ease you back a step. Pro users also get body signals: short sleep or a very active day keeps today's fast where it is, and low glucose readings during recent fasts ease you back.

Why don't I see the AI Coach?

The AI Coach uses Apple Intelligence on your device. It appears on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on, in a language the on-device model supports. Otherwise TimetoEat shows the written explanation instead.

Why is my plan capped?

Your health check sets a maximum fast length. You can review your answers in Settings → Health check.

Glucose isn't showing

Glucose comes from Apple Health. Many sensor apps write to Apple Health with a delay of up to several hours. TimetoEat never alerts you about glucose; use your meter or sensor app for real-time readings.

Restore TimetoEat Pro

Open Settings → Upgrade to Pro → Restore purchases. Manage your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Move to a new iPhone

With iCloud turned on, your fasts and settings sync automatically through your private iCloud. You can also export your history from Settings → Export fasting history (CSV).
