Support

How does the script follow my voice?

Choose Voice in the Studio (it's the default). SelfTake listens on your device and keeps the line you're reading right under the camera. If you pause or ad-lib, it waits. Voice-Track works offline in more than 50 languages, including Hebrew, Arabic and Thai; in other languages the script scrolls while you talk and stops when you stop.

Add a script from another app

Share text or a text file (TXT, Markdown, RTF, HTML) from Notes, Pages, Mail, Safari or Files and choose SelfTake. You can also tap the import button in the Scripts list, paste text, or use “Open in SelfTake” on a file.

Use the Apple Watch as a remote

Install SelfTake on your Watch from the Watch app on iPhone. Open SelfTake on both devices. Tap record (or double-tap with your fingers on supported Watch models), turn the Digital Crown to change the speed, and jump between sections.

Other remotes

Bluetooth presenters and foot pedals that act as keyboards work out of the box: Space/Return starts and stops, arrows move the script. The volume buttons, Camera Control and AirPods (stem press) also start and stop recording.

Mirror mode for teleprompter rigs

Tap the mirror button in the Studio, or turn on Mirror horizontally in Settings, to read the script through a beam-splitter glass.

Free version limits

Free: unlimited scripts, mirror mode, right-to-left text, Voice-Track, takes up to 60 seconds at 1080p, export with no watermark, and basic AI help (3 a day). Pro removes the limits.

Restore or cancel Pro

Open Settings in SelfTake › Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Why don't I see AI script help?

It uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It appears on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, with it turned on, in a language the on-device model supports. Fit-to-Time works in every language.
