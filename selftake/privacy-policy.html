Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Applies to SelfTake: Teleprompter Camera for iPhone, Apple Watch, Android phones and Wear OS watches

The short version

SelfTake has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We don't run servers that receive your data. Your scripts, videos, voice and face stay on your devices. We can't see them.

Camera and microphone

SelfTake uses the camera and microphone only to record the videos you choose to make, and to scroll your script as you speak. Recordings are saved inside the app on your iPhone until you delete them or save them to Photos.

Speech recognition

Voice-Track and captions use Apple's speech recognition on your device. Your voice is not sent to us or to Apple's servers for this. Apple may download language files to your device the first time you use a language.

Face detection and touch-up

To place the face light and smooth skin, SelfTake detects where a face is in each frame on your device. It does not identify who you are, and it stores no face data.

AI script help

AI script help uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Your script is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on, your scripts and settings sync through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they're available on your other devices. Only you can access this data; we have no access to it. Videos are not uploaded to iCloud by SelfTake.

Photos and files

SelfTake only adds videos to your photo library when you tap Save, and only reads files you choose to import or share into it. It never reads your existing photos.

Apple Watch

The Watch app sends remote-control commands (record, stop, speed) to your iPhone over Apple's Watch connection. No content leaves your devices.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales reports with developers.

SelfTake for Android

The Android app works the same way, with Google's on-device technology in place of Apple's:

  • Speech recognition: Voice-Track and captions use Google ML Kit's speech recognition on your phone where it's available, or a speech model that the app downloads once from Google Play. Your voice is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or to Google for this.
  • AI script help: uses Google's on-device model (Gemini Nano) through ML Kit, on phones that support it. Your script is processed on your phone.
  • Face detection and touch-up: done on your phone to place the face light and smooth skin in the camera preview and in the videos you edit and save. It does not identify who you are and stores no face data.
  • Backup: if Android backup is turned on, your scripts and settings are included in your Google account's backup. Videos are only copied when you move to a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer.
  • Wear OS: the watch app sends remote-control commands to your phone over Google's watch connection. No content leaves your devices.
  • Purchases: processed by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.
  • Diagnostics sent by Google ML Kit: the ML Kit libraries the app uses for speech, language detection and AI send Google technical information so Google can keep them working: device and app information (model, Android and app version), performance metrics, error codes, the language settings used, and per-installation identifiers. It is encrypted in transit, used by Google for diagnostics and usage analytics, and not shared with us or with third parties. It never includes your voice, videos or scripts. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Data we collect

None. SelfTake does not collect, store or share personal data on any server. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. On Android, the only data that leaves your phone is the ML Kit diagnostics described above, which go to Google, not to us; the Google Play Data safety section lists them.

Children

SelfTake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete any script or video in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all data stored by it on that device. To remove synced scripts, delete them in the app or manage iCloud storage in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
